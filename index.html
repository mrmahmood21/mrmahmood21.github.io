About Me

Mechanical engineer with experience in robotic systems across hardware, software, controls, electronics, sensing, and modeling — for building new systems as well as improving current ones.

At CMU, my work focuses on lower-limb exoskeletons and human-in-the-loop control. I have developed a real-time, physics-based, data-free human joint torque estimator running on an NVIDIA Jetson with multiple IMUs, and I am currently testing the task space where it performs better than or comparably to a data-driven estimator. I am also building toward VLM-based, context-informed exoskeleton assistance to improve user personalization and comfort.

I am especially interested in robot control and robot learning for wearable robotics, humanoids, and human-robot interaction. I aspire to help bridge the gap between robotics, AI, and people, leaving a positive impact on the world.

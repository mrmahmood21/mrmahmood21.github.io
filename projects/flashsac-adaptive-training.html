FlashSAC Replication and Adaptive Training

Reproducing FlashSAC, a fast and stable off-policy soft actor-critic algorithm, on quadruped locomotion in Isaac Lab, and extending its fixed training schedule for long-horizon tasks.

Timeline
October 2026 – Present
Context
Robot Learning, Carnegie Mellon University
Tools
Python, PyTorch, Isaac Lab
Status
In progress

Approach

  • Reproducing FlashSAC, a fast and stable off-policy soft actor-critic algorithm for high-dimensional robot control, on quadruped locomotion tasks in Isaac Lab
  • Extending its fixed training schedule for long-horizon tasks with prioritized replay sampling (PER, ERE, LaBER) in place of uniform sampling, and an actor-update delay that adapts to critic reliability
  • Benchmarking the extensions against the original FlashSAC and a prioritized-replay baseline

FlashSAC Replication and Adaptive Training

Timeline
October 2026 – Present
Context
Robot Learning, Carnegie Mellon University
Tools
Software Python, PyTorch, Isaac Lab

Legged robots increasingly learn to walk in simulation through reinforcement learning, but that training is slow, and long-horizon tasks remain hard to learn reliably. FlashSAC is a recent off-policy soft actor-critic algorithm designed to make such training fast and stable for high-dimensional robot control. As one of six members of a Robot Learning course team, I am helping reproduce FlashSAC on quadruped locomotion tasks in Isaac Lab. Building on that replication, our objective is to extend its fixed training schedule for long-horizon tasks in two ways: prioritized replay sampling (PER, ERE, and LaBER) in place of uniform sampling, and an actor-update delay that adapts to how reliable the critic is. Both extensions will be benchmarked against the original FlashSAC and a standard prioritized-replay baseline.

Controls Education with Maglev

A PID-controlled, IR-sensed maglev rig for interactive controls demonstrations, with gains selected from a nonlinear Simulink model and a 3D-printed SolidWorks housing.

Timeline
October 2025 – December 2025
Tools
C++, PID Control, Simulink, SolidWorks

What I Built

  • Led programming of a PID controller for an IR-sensed maglev system and tuned gains for stable position control
  • Modeled nonlinear system dynamics of levitating magnets in Simulink to select gains for levitation both above and below the device
  • Programmed an Arduino user interface with an LCD screen, knobs, and buttons for interactive control demonstrations
  • Iterated 3D-printed housing designs in SolidWorks to constrain the magnet to smooth vertical motion
